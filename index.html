Multimodal Breast Tumor Ultrasound Video Segmentation Benchmark Dataset

This demo presents frame-level ultrasound images, sparsely annotated masks, per-frame overlay images, and composited overlay videos for BI-RADS L2–L5 grade breast tumor ultrasound videos. For each case video, both the original diagnostic report and a structured report cleaned by the Lingshu32B model are provided. Two representative case videos are included for each grade.
